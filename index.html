About Us

Who We Are

Sol Foreign Employment Agency P.L.C is a licensed recruitment agency based in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. We specialize in supplying professional housemaids and skilled workers to Saudi Arabia, UAE, Kuwait, and other Gulf countries.

Government Licensed

Fully licensed by the Ethiopian Ministry of Labor and compliant with all Saudi recruitment regulations including Musaned.

Ethical Recruitment

We follow zero-fee recruitment principles. Workers never pay for placement. Fair wages and proper contracts guaranteed.

Verified & Trained

Every housemaid undergoes thorough background checks, medical screening (GAMCA), and pre-departure training.

End-to-End Service

From sourcing in Ethiopia to airport departure and post-arrival support in Saudi Arabia — we handle everything.

What We Offer

Comprehensive housemaid recruitment services tailored for Saudi Arabian families and employers.

Live-in Housemaids

Nanny & Childcare
